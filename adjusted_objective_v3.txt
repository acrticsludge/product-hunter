Find real, recurring, evidence-backed pain points that can be solved as a website or web service — things that make everyday life easier for individuals and small teams. Target problems where people repeatedly: (1) do manual or repetitive administrative work online, (2) struggle with fragmented tools that do not integrate with each other, (3) cannot find or book appointments, services, or slots easily, (4) manage personal data across scattered spreadsheets and email, (5) deal with confusing government or business processes, (6) track subscriptions, renewals, deadlines, or appointments manually, (7) compare services or prices across providers, or (8) coordinate with others through scattered messages and threads.

For each candidate, require evidence: pain is genuinely recurring (multiple independent first-hand reports); existing solutions are inadequate; people are spending time or money on workarounds; the problem is narrow enough for a small team to build as a website service; there is willingness-to-pay evidence. Prioritize complaints with existing workarounds, repeated frustration, failed attempts at existing tools, or active spending evidence.

Search across Hacker News, GitHub Issues, dev.to, Reddit. Prefer independent first-hand evidence and failed-solution or alternative discussions over generic ideas. Aim for 5-10 strong website-service opportunities with discriminative confidence, not many weak ideas.

Use all quality gates normally. Do not lower gates or manufacture evidence. Variant: third probe with clustering and validation fallback fixes enabled for gate validation testing.
